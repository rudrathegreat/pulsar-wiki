## Handover status

No research handover has been published yet. Use the **Project Handover** workspace in the application to select relevant wiki pages and source material, generate an AI-organised draft, and review it before publishing.

## What the reviewed guide will capture

- The project purpose, scope, current state, and key results.
- Relevant papers, annotations, notebooks, wiki pages, data, and supporting materials.
- Important methodologies, computational analyses, assumptions, limitations, and decisions.
- Outstanding research questions, unresolved issues, risks, and prioritised next steps.
- Links to original wiki pages and source filenames so future researchers can trace the evidence.

AI-generated content is a draft. A researcher or supervisor must verify its claims, references, and omissions before it can replace this template.
