# Polarimetry

**Summary**: The study of the polarization state of pulsar radiation, providing insights into the magnetic field structure and emission geometry.

**Sources**: [[psrchive.pdf]], [[pulsar-magnetosphere.md]]

**Last updated**: 2026-04-27

---

Pulsar emission is often highly polarized, sometimes reaching 100% linear polarization. Measuring this is critical for understanding the [[pulsar-magnetosphere]] (source: psrchive.pdf).

## Stokes Parameters
The polarization state is described by four Stokes parameters:
- **$I$**: Total intensity.
- **$Q, U$**: Linear polarization components.
- **$V$**: Circular polarization.

## The Rotating Vector Model (RVM)
As the pulsar rotates, the position angle of the linear polarization often follows an S-shaped curve. This is well-described by the RVM, which allows astronomers to determine the geometry of the emission beam and the orientation of the pulsar's rotation and magnetic axes (source: psrchive.pdf).

## Faraday Rotation
As polarized radio waves travel through the magnetized interstellar medium, their plane of polarization rotates. Measuring the **Rotation Measure (RM)** allows pulsar researchers to probe the Galactic magnetic field.

## Calibration
Instruments often introduce polarization artifacts. Software like [[psrchive]] is used to perform rigorous polarimetric calibration to recover the true astrophysical signal (source: psrchive.pdf).
