# Post-Keplerian Parameters

**Summary**: Relativistic corrections to the Keplerian orbital description of a binary pulsar, measured through pulse-arrival-time variations.

**Sources**: [1011.0718.pdf](/#file/1011.0718.pdf), [tempo2_manual.pdf](/#file/tempo2_manual.pdf), [s41550-019-0880-2 (1).pdf](/#file/s41550-019-0880-2%20%281%29.pdf)

**Last updated**: 2026-10-23

---

In a relativistic binary, post-Keplerian (PK) parameters describe effects such as periastron advance, gravitational redshift and time dilation, orbital-period decay, and Shapiro delay. Measuring multiple PK parameters over-constrains the component masses and tests the theory used to predict them (source: [1011.0718.pdf](/#file/1011.0718.pdf); [s41550-019-0880-2 (1).pdf](/#file/s41550-019-0880-2%20%281%29.pdf)).

For PSR B1913+16, the periastron-advance and gravitational-redshift parameters determine the total mass and the component masses. The orbital-period derivative then provides an independent test of gravitational-radiation damping after correcting for Galactic acceleration and proper-motion effects (source: [1011.0718.pdf](/#file/1011.0718.pdf)).

The timing model also includes the Shapiro propagation delay, although the 2010 analysis discussed future prospects for a robust measurement of its range and shape in this system (source: [1011.0718.pdf](/#file/1011.0718.pdf)).
