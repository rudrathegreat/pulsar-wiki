# Radio Pulsar

**Summary**: The most common type of pulsar, emitting periodic bursts of radio waves through a "lighthouse" beam mechanism.

**Sources**: [pulsars.md](/#file/pulsars.md)

**Last updated**: 2026-04-27

---

Radio pulsars are highly magnetized, rapidly rotating [[neutron-star]]s. They produce a beam of radiation that sweeps across the Earth as the star rotates, much like a lighthouse (source: [pulsars.md](/#file/pulsars.md)).

## Characteristics
- **Spin Rate**: Most rotate about once per second.
- **[[millisecond-pulsar]]s**: Pulsars with periods shorter than ~50 milliseconds.
- **Evolution**: As they emit energy, their rotation frequency slows down until they eventually cease radiating (source: [pulsars.md](/#file/pulsars.md)).

## Associations
Some radio pulsars are found within supernova remnants, confirming their origin as stellar remnants.
