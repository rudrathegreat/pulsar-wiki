# MeerKAT

**Summary**: A 64-antenna radio interferometer in South Africa that serves as a premier facility for high-cadence, high-precision pulsar timing.

**Sources**: [[stac3719.pdf]]

**Last updated**: 2026-04-27

---

MeerKAT is located in the Karoo region of South Africa and is a precursor to the Square Kilometre Array (SKA). It is renowned for its high sensitivity and wide-band receivers (source: stac3719.pdf).

## The MeerTime Project
MeerTime is a five-year project dedicated to the timing of over 1,000 pulsars using MeerKAT. It is divided into several sub-themes:
1. **PTA (Pulsar Timing Array)**: High-cadence monitoring of MSPs to detect gravitational waves (source: [[pulsar-timing-array]]).
2. **Relativistic Binary (RelBin)**: Studying systems where strong-field gravity effects like [[shapiro-delay]] are visible.
3. **Pulsar Searches**: Finding new pulsars in the southern sky (source: stac3719.pdf).

## Instrumentation & Receivers
- **UHF Receiver**: Operates from 544 to 1088 MHz. Its lower frequency range is excellent for measuring [[dispersion-measure]] variations.
- **L-band Receiver**: Operates from 856 to 1712 MHz. This is the standard band for high-precision timing of [[millisecond-pulsar]]s.
- **PTUSE Backend**: The Pulsar Timing User Specifiable Equipment (PTUSE) allows for coherent de-dispersion and real-time data folding (source: stac3719.pdf).

## Key Research Contributions
Recent MeerKAT observations have significantly improved the mass constraints for several binary MSPs, utilizing advanced parameterizations of the [[shapiro-delay]] (source: stac3719.pdf).
