# Crab Pulsar

**Summary**: A famous optical and radio pulsar located in the Crab Nebula, the remnant of a supernova observed in 1054 AD.

**Sources**: [[pulsars.md]]

**Last updated**: 2026-04-27

---

The Crab Pulsar is one of the most studied objects in astronomy. It is the remnant of the SN 1054 supernova explosion (source: pulsars.md).

## Observational Features
- **Multi-wavelength**: It is one of the few pulsars visible at radio, optical, X-ray, and gamma-ray wavelengths.
- **Optical Subset**: It belongs to a very small subset of known optical pulsars (source: pulsars.md).
