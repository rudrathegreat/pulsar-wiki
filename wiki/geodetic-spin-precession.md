# Geodetic Spin Precession

**Summary**: Relativistic precession of a spinning body’s orientation as it moves through the curved spacetime associated with its binary orbit.

**Sources**: [1011.0718.pdf](/#file/1011.0718.pdf)

**Last updated**: 2026-10-23

---

In a relativistic binary pulsar, geodetic spin precession changes the orientation of the pulsar's spin axis relative to the observer. Because the radio beam is tied to the magnetic and spin geometry, the effect can produce secular changes in the observed pulse profile (source: [1011.0718.pdf](/#file/1011.0718.pdf)).

For PSR B1913+16, the paper interprets long-term pulse-shape evolution in this framework and discusses how continued profile monitoring can constrain the geometry. The interpretation depends on beam and viewing-geometry modelling; profile evolution alone is not a direct measurement of the spin vector (source: [1011.0718.pdf](/#file/1011.0718.pdf)).
