# Magnetar

**Summary**: An exotic type of [[neutron-star]] characterized by extremely powerful magnetic fields ($10^{14}$ to $10^{15}$ Gauss) that power high-energy emission.

**Sources**: [[pulsars.md]]

**Last updated**: 2026-04-27

---

Magnetars are a distinct subset of the [[pulsar]] population where the primary energy source is the decay of an intense magnetic field, rather than rotation (source: pulsars.md).

## Physical Nature
Magnetars possess the strongest magnetic fields known in the universe. These fields are so intense they can distort the shapes of atoms and lead to starquakes on the neutron star's crust.

## Observational Characteristics
- **Anomalous X-ray Pulsars (AXPs)**: A class of magnetars that emit steady, intense X-rays.
- **Soft Gamma Repeaters (SGRs)**: Magnetars known for producing enormous bursts of gamma rays and X-rays.
- **Radio Emission**: While originally thought to be radio-quiet, some magnetars have been observed to emit transient radio pulses (source: pulsars.md).
