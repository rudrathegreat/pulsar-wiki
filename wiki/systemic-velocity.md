# Systemic Velocity

**Summary**: The space motion of a pulsar binary's center of mass, inferred from proper motion, distance, and Galactic kinematics.

**Sources**: [1011.0718.pdf](/#file/1011.0718.pdf)

**Last updated**: 2026-10-23

---

A binary pulsar's systemic velocity combines its measured proper motion with its distance and the adopted solar and Galactic motions. It is distinct from the pulsar's orbital velocity around its companion (source: [1011.0718.pdf](/#file/1011.0718.pdf)).

For PSR B1913+16, the paper estimates planar and polar velocity components of approximately 247 and 51 km s\(^{-1}\), respectively, relative to the pulsar's own standard of rest. The inferred velocity is comparatively large among well-measured double-neutron-star systems, subject to the adopted distance and Galactic model (source: [1011.0718.pdf](/#file/1011.0718.pdf)).
